# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Model
@m_titel
Model: assumptions, calculation, limits
@m_annahmen_titel
Assumptions (as on the self-check page)
@m_annahmen
<ol><li>The contents of the cup are ideally mixed; the milk mixes at once and without heat loss.</li><li>No water vapour escapes under the lid; the mass of the contents stays constant.</li><li>The total heat loss through wall, bottom and lid is $\dot{Q}_\mathrm{ab} = kA\,(T - T_\mathrm{u})$ with constant $kA$.</li><li>The room temperature is constant.</li><li>The heat capacity of cup and lid is negligible.</li><li>Coffee (like water) and milk are incompressible with constant properties.</li></ol>
@m_rechnung_titel
How the page calculates
@m_rechnung
<p>The same equations as the worksheet, time in seconds. Balance for the contents of one cup:</p>$$m\,c_p\,\frac{\mathrm{d}\vartheta}{\mathrm{d}t} = -kA\,(\vartheta - \vartheta_\mathrm{u})$$<p>Solution with the initial temperature $\vartheta_0$:</p>$$\vartheta(t) = \vartheta_\mathrm{u} + (\vartheta_0 - \vartheta_\mathrm{u})\exp\left(-\frac{kA}{m\,c_p}\,t\right)$$<p>Mixing without heat loss:</p>$$\vartheta_{1,0} = \frac{C_\mathrm{K}\,\vartheta_\mathrm{K,0} + C_\mathrm{M}\,\vartheta_\mathrm{M}}{C_\mathrm{K} + C_\mathrm{M}}$$<p>Heat released until the milk is added: $Q = C\,(\vartheta_0 - \vartheta)$.</p>
@m_pruef
<b>Check values from the worksheet:</b> $kA$ = {{z:kA}} W/K from {{e:thMess}} °C after 10 min (d, provisional); cup 1 reaches {{e:trink}} °C after {{z:t_tr}} min (h1).
@m_grenzen_titel
Limits
@m_grenzen
<ul><li>The cup stores heat: in the experiment the mixing temperature is higher and the difference smaller.</li><li>$kA$ is not quite constant.</li><li>Without a lid, evaporation adds to the heat loss (part j); the page does not calculate it.</li><li>The page shows calculated values, not measured values.</li></ul>
